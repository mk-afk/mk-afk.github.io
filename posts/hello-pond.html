Hello, pond

June 12, 2026

This site needed a reason to exist beyond holding a resume, so I gave it one: a pond. Everything swimming on the home page is procedural. No sprites, no video files, just a canvas and some geometry.

Each koi is a chain of 26 points. The head steers, and every point behind it follows the one ahead at a fixed distance. That constraint is nearly the whole skeleton, three lines:

const d = Math.hypot(dx, dy) || 1;
cx[i] = cx[i-1] + (dx / d) * segLen;
cy[i] = cy[i-1] + (dy / d) * segLen;

One more rule keeps the fish honest: no joint may bend more than about fourteen degrees, so a koi can curl into a C but never fold back over itself.

A sine wave travels down the spine to make it swim, the body is an outline drawn at varying widths along those points, the fins are fans pinned to it, and depth is faked by tinting everything toward the water color. The markings are blots laid down at random, once per fish, so every visitor gets koi nobody else has seen.

I’ll write here occasionally about whatever I’m building or reading. No schedule. The fish don’t keep one either.
